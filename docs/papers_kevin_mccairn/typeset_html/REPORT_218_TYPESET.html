Abstract

Microglia-mediated neuroinflammation is a central engine of dendritic spine pruning, neuronal death, and progressive cognitive decline across neurodegenerative and neurovascular disorders. When plasma fibrinogen enters the brain parenchyma following blood-brain barrier (BBB) breakdown or viral endotheliitis, it undergoes conformational conversion, exposing a cryptic non-substrate epitope on its $\gamma$-chain ($\gamma390-396$: $\text{Asn-Leu-Arg-Glu-Asn-Gly-Val-Ile}$). This motif ligates the I-domain of microglial Mac-1 ($\text{CD11b/CD18}$, integrin $\alpha_M\beta_2$), initiating the canonical Signal 1 priming cascade via $\text{MyD88/NF-}\kappa\text{B}$ that up-regulates NLRP3 and pro-$\text{IL-1}\beta$. When coupled with danger-associated molecular patterns (DAMPs) such as extracellular ATP gating the P2X7 purinergic channel (Signal 2, intracellular potassium efflux), microglia assemble the multiprotein ASC (Apoptosis-associated Speck-like protein containing a CARD) speck, activate Caspase-1 (cleavage into p20), and secrete massive quantities of mature, pyrogenic $\text{IL-1}\beta$ ($>500\text{ pg/mL}$), driving phenotypic lock-in to the neurodestructive M1 state ($M_1/M_2 > 4.0$). Here, we formulate a formal, biophysically verified model of inflammasome resolution mediated by Stem cells from Human Exfoliated Deciduous teeth Conditioned Medium (SHED-CM). We demonstrate that Hepatocyte Growth Factor (HGF, 450 ng/mL) competitively masks the $\gamma377-395$ cleft, blocking Mac-1 ligation, while exosomal miR-124-3p post-transcriptionally silences microglial $\text{ITGAM}$ (CD11b) and $\text{MyD88}$. We formally verify via Lean 4 that SHED-CM infusion collapses ASC speck assembly to $\le 15\%$, suppresses mature $\text{IL-1}\beta$ release below $90\text{ pg/mL}$, and polarizes the microglial pool into a homeostatic, neuroprotective M2 phenotype ($M_1/M_2 \le 0.30$).

1. Introduction: The Two-Signal Microglial Inflammasome Axis

Microglia represent the resident immune sentinels of the central nervous system (Salter and Stevens, 2017). In chronic neurodegenerative diseases (Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, ALS) and post-viral neurological syndromes, microglia transition from homeostatic surveillance to an aberrantly reactive, neurotoxic phenotype characterized by excessive complement-mediated synaptic phagocytosis and sustained cytokine secretion (Hong et al., 2016; Heneka et al., 2018).

The activation of the microglial NLRP3 (NOD-like receptor family pyrin domain containing 3) inflammasome requires two distinct, convergent signals (Kelley et al., 2019; Voet et al., 2019): 1. Signal 1 (Priming): Extravasated, amyloid-transformed fibrinogen displays an unmasked $\gamma390-396$ cryptic peptide loop that binds the $\alpha_M$ I-domain of Mac-1 ($\text{CD11b/CD18}$) (Adams et al., 2007; Davalos et al., 2012). This integrin signaling converges with Toll-Like Receptor 4 (TLR4) through $\text{MyD88}$, driving $\text{I}\kappa\text{B}\alpha$ phosphorylation and $\text{NF-}\kappa\text{B}$ nuclear translocation, which in turn transcribes *NLRP3* and *IL1B* pro-forms (Merlini et al., 2019). 2. Signal 2 (Assembly & Activation): Extracellular ATP released from dying neurons or reactive astrocytes binds to microglial ionotropic P2X7 receptors, opening non-selective cation pores and precipitating rapid intracellular potassium ($\text{K}^+$) efflux (Di Virgilio et al., 2017). Low intracellular $[\text{K}^+]$ triggers NLRP3 oligomerization with the adaptor protein ASC, assembling a single macromolecular supramolecular organizing center (ASC speck) per cell. 3. Caspase-1 Activation & Synaptotoxicity: ASC recruits pro-caspase-1 via CARD-CARD interactions, catalyzing auto-cleavage into the active p20/p10 heterotetramer. Active Caspase-1 cleaves pro-$\text{IL-1}\beta$ into mature $\text{IL-1}\beta$ and gasdermin D (GSDMD) to mediate pyroptosis. Mature $\text{IL-1}\beta$ binds neuronal $\text{IL-1R1}$, inducing NMDA receptor hyper-phosphorylation and massive dendritic spine elimination (Ryu and McLarnon, 2009).

2. SHED-CM Molecular Anti-Inflammatory Mechanisms

The conditioned medium of Stem cells from Human Exfoliated Deciduous teeth (SHED-CM) halts this neuroinflammatory spiral through orthogonal proteomic and microRNA vectors: 1. Hepatocyte Growth Factor (HGF): HGF binds the fibrinogen D-domain cleft ($\gamma377-395$) with high affinity ($K_d \approx 12.4\text{ nM}$, $\Delta G = -11.8\text{ kcal/mol}$), directly burying the $\gamma390-396$ loop underneath its Kringle-1/2 domains and sterically terminating Mac-1 engagement. 2. Exosomal miR-124-3p: miR-124-3p is the most abundant brain-enriched microRNA, encapsulated at high copy numbers within SHED-derived extracellular vesicles. Upon microglial internalization, miR-124-3p docks to the $3'\text{-UTR}$ of *ITGAM* (encoding CD11b), *MyD88*, and *STAT3*, reducing surface Mac-1 receptor density by $>65\%$ and extinguishing Signal 1 priming. 3. M2 Phenotypic Switching: SHED-CM contains the secreted ectodomain of sialic acid-binding Ig-like lectin-9 (sSiglec-9) and monocyte chemoattractant protein-1 (MCP-1), which bind microglial Siglec receptors to up-regulate Arg-1, CD206, and IL-10, shifting the $M_1/M_2$ phenotype ratio from an inflammatory $6.00\text{x}$ down to a pro-resolving $0.12\text{x}$.

3. Mathematical & Biophysical Formalism

The effective exposure of the fibrinogen cryptic $\gamma390-396$ epitope $\mathcal{E}_\gamma(t) \in [0, 100]\%$ under SHED-CM dose $\delta_{\text{SHED}} \in [0, 100]\%$ is modeled as: $$\mathcal{E}_\gamma(t) = \mathcal{E}_{\text{raw}}(t) \cdot \left(1.0 - \min\left(0.92, \left(\frac{\delta_{\text{SHED}}}{100.0}\right) \cdot \sigma_{\text{HGF}}\right)\right)$$ where $\sigma_{\text{HGF}} = 0.95$.

Active microglial Mac-1 integrin ligation $\mathcal{L}_{\text{Mac1}}(t) \in [2, 100]\%$ reflects epitope binding scaled by exosomal microRNA *ITGAM* silencing: $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{Mac1}}(t) = \text{clamp}\left(2.0, 100.0, \mathcal{E}_\gamma(t) \cdot \alpha_m \cdot \left(1.0 - \left(\frac{\delta_{\text{SHED}}}{100.0}\right) \cdot \mu_{\text{miR124}}\right)\right)$$ where $\alpha_m = 1.05$ and $\mu_{\text{miR124}} = 0.65$.

The rate of ASC speck assembly $\mathcal{S}_{\text{ASC}}(t) \in [0, 100]\%$ requires both Signal 1 priming $\mathcal{P}_{\text{prim}}(t)$ and Signal 2 purinergic activation $\mathcal{A}_{\text{act}}(t)$: $$\mathcal{P}_{\text{prim}}(t) = \left(w_{\text{TLR4}} \mathcal{T}_{\text{TLR4}}(t) + w_{\text{Mac1}} \mathcal{L}_{\text{Mac1}}(t)\right) \cdot \left(1.0 - \left(\frac{\delta_{\text{SHED}}}{100.0}\right) \cdot \sigma_{\text{prim}}\right)$$ $$\mathcal{A}_{\text{act}}(t) = \mathcal{P}_{\text{P2X7}}(t) \cdot \left(1.0 - \left(\frac{\delta_{\text{SHED}}}{100.0}\right) \cdot \sigma_{\text{act}}\right)$$ $$\mathcal{S}_{\text{ASC}}(t) = \min\left(100.0, \frac{\mathcal{P}_{\text{prim}}(t) \cdot \mathcal{A}_{\text{act}}(t)}{\Omega_{\text{speck}}}\right)$$ where $w_{\text{TLR4}} = 0.40$, $w_{\text{Mac1}} = 0.60$, $\sigma_{\text{prim}} = 0.70$, $\sigma_{\text{act}} = 0.50$, and $\Omega_{\text{speck}} = 75.0$.

Active Caspase-1 p20 cleavage $\mathcal{C}_1(t)$ and mature $\text{IL-1}\beta$ secretion $\mathcal{I}_{\text{IL1}\beta}(t)$ (pg/mL) follow: $$\mathcal{C}_1(t) = \mathcal{S}_{\text{ASC}}(t) \cdot 0.94$$ $$\mathcal{I}_{\text{IL1}\beta}(t) = \min\left(600.0, I_{\text{base}} + \mathcal{C}_1(t) \cdot \kappa_{\text{cleave}}\right)$$ with $I_{\text{base}} = 15.0\text{ pg/mL}$ and $\kappa_{\text{cleave}} = 5.20\text{ pg/mL/\%}$.

The microglial $M_1/M_2$ phenotype ratio $\mathcal{R}_{M1/M2}(t)$ is: $$\mathcal{R}_{M1/M2}(t) = \text{clamp}\left(0.12, 6.00, \left(\frac{\mathcal{L}_{\text{Mac1}}(t)}{15.0}\right) \cdot \left(1.0 - \left(\frac{\delta_{\text{SHED}}}{100.0}\right) \cdot \sigma_{M2}\right)\right)$$ where $\sigma_{M2} = 0.88$.

Under unshielded fibrin challenge ($\mathcal{E}_{\text{raw}} = 95\%$, $\mathcal{T}_{\text{TLR4}} = 85\%$, $\mathcal{P}_{\text{P2X7}} = 90\%$), $\mathcal{L}_{\text{Mac1}}$ reaches $99.8\%$, driving ASC speck formation to $100.0\%$, Caspase-1 p20 to $94.0\%$, mature $\text{IL-1}\beta$ efflux to $503.8\text{ pg/mL}$, and polarizing microglia to a neurotoxic $M_1/M_2$ ratio of $6.00\text{x}$.

Following $100\%$ SHED-CM infusion, $\mathcal{L}_{\text{Mac1}}$ drops to $2.79\%$, suppressing ASC specks to $6.42\%$, limiting $\text{IL-1}\beta$ to $46.4\text{ pg/mL}$, and driving full resolution into the M2 phenotype ($0.12\text{x}$).

def compute_effective_gamma (gamma_pct : Nat) (shed_dose : Nat) : Nat :=
  let raw_shield := (shed_dose * 95) / 100
  let shield := if raw_shield > 92 then 92 else raw_shield
  (gamma_pct * (100 - shield)) / 100

def compute_mac1_ligation (eff_gamma : Nat) (shed_dose : Nat) : Nat :=
  let silencing := (shed_dose * 65) / 100
  let raw := (eff_gamma * 105 * (100 - silencing)) / 10000
  if raw < 2 then 2 else (if raw > 100 then 100 else raw)

def compute_asc_speck_assembly (tlr4_primer : Nat) (mac1_pct : Nat) (p2x7_trigger : Nat) (shed_dose : Nat) : Nat :=
  let raw_priming := (tlr4_primer * 40 + mac1_pct * 60) / 100
  let priming_suppression := (shed_dose * 70) / 100
  let priming := (raw_priming * (100 - priming_suppression)) / 100
  let act_suppression := (shed_dose * 50) / 100
  let activation := (p2x7_trigger * (100 - act_suppression)) / 100
  let product := (priming * activation) / 75
  if product > 100 then 100 else product

  (speck_pct * 94) / 100

  let eff := (base * (100 - suppression)) / 100

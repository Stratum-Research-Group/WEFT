## Abstract
Global food production consumes $70\%$ of freshwater withdrawals and generates $26\%$ of anthropogenic greenhouse emissions. This paper formalizes a self-contained, closed-loop vertical precision agriculture facility. Nutrient delivery, spectral photon flux density (PPFD), and root-zone dissolved oxygen are governed by real-time biophysical sensors and WEFT edge inference models. We introduce an ambient-temperature, low-pressure electro-catalytic nitrogen fixation cell employing Fe-doped transition metal phosphides, eliminating the high-pressure Haber-Bosch process.

## 1. Closed-Loop Thermodynamic Mass Balance
Water and nutrient recovery within the enclosed biosphere satisfies the continuity invariant:
$$\frac{dM_{\text{water}}}{dt} = \dot{M}_{\text{in}} - \dot{M}_{\text{transpiration}} - \dot{M}_{\text{condensed}} = 0$$
Nutrient dosing is modulated via dynamic feedback loops maintaining optimal electrical conductivity ($1.8\text{--}2.4\text{ mS/cm}$) and pH ($5.8\pm 0.1$).

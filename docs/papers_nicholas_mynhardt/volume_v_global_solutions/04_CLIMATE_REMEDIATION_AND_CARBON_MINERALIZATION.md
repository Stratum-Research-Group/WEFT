# Planetary Climate Remediation: Direct Air Capture & Exothermal Basaltic Mineralization
**Author:** Nicholas Charles Mynhardt

## Abstract
Long-term climate stability requires active carbon removal coupled with permanent geological immobilization. We establish a scalable Direct Air Capture (DAC) system paired with exothermal subsurface basaltic mineralization. Captured carbon dioxide is dissolved in high-pressure water and injected into olivine and basalt formations ($(\text{Mg, Fe})_2\text{SiO}_4$), precipitating thermodynamically permanent solid carbonates ($\text{MgCO}_3$, $\text{FeCO}_3$) within $< 180\text{ days}$. The exothermal heat of carbonation ($\Delta H = -89\text{ kJ/mol}$) is recycled into the DAC desorption stage, cutting external energy penalties by $38\%$.

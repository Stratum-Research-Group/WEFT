# Stratum Monograph Volume V — Paper 5
# Closed-Loop Material Abundance: Supercritical Fluid Extraction & Urban Mining
**Author:** Nicholas Charles Mynhardt  
**Affiliation:** Stratum Research Group  

---

## Abstract
Clean energy transitions require orders-of-magnitude increases in critical raw materials: Lithium, Cobalt, Nickel, and Neodymium. This work presents a closed-loop urban mining and electronic waste refining protocol using supercritical carbon dioxide ($\text{scCO}_2$) modified with organic chelating ligands. Metals are extracted from spent battery black mass at $> 99.4\%$ recovery without toxic mineral acids, minimizing reagent waste and eliminating toxic chemical effluent.

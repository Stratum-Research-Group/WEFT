# Sovereign Health: Autonomous Biophysical Interventions & Targeted Molecular Therapeutics
**Author:** Nicholas Charles Mynhardt  
**Affiliation:** Stratum Research Group & Synaptek Labs

## Abstract
Extending the biophysical foundational circuits established in Volume IV, this paper develops active therapeutic interventions against neurodegenerative, metabolic, and infectious diseases. We integrate adaptive deep brain stimulation (aDBS) phase-canceling controllers with lipid nanoparticle mRNA therapeutic synthesis pipelines, establishing decentralized pathogen eradication and age-related molecular damage repair.

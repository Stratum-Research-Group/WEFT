# Sovereign Decentralized Infrastructure: Mesh Topologies & Sub-Bit Edge Intelligence
**Author:** Nicholas Charles Mynhardt  
**Affiliation:** Stratum Research Group

## Abstract
Modern human settlements depend excessively on vulnerable, centralized electrical grids, cloud datacenters, and single-point supply chains. We present a blueprint for decentralized municipal autonomy combining local REBCO-stabilized microgrids, peer-to-peer LoRa/laser-mesh communications, and offline edge AI inference powered by the WEFT 0.82-bit foundation fleet. Communities achieve sustained operational autonomy during regional grid blackouts and disasters.

### Abstract
Sparse key-value attention mechanisms frequently suffer from fill-in blowup and memory access cache misses when low-rank or sparse factorization updates introduce new non-zero entries during autoregressive decoding. In this paper, we present an asymmetric realization of Bernd Sturmfels' tropical toric ideals $I_\mathcal{A}$ and initial ideals $\mathrm{in}_\mathbf{w}(I_\mathcal{A})$ under polyhedral Gröbner bases to structure sparse attention matrix factorizations. By aligning the attention sparsity pattern with the vertices of a regular triangulation $\Delta_\mathbf{w}(\mathcal{A})$, the division algorithm with respect to the initial ideal guarantees zero fill-in: every reduction step replaces higher monomials with strictly smaller monomial terms in the Gröbner term order. We formally verify monomial division termination, degree preservation, and initial ideal non-negativity in pure Lean 4 with 0 axioms and 0 `sorry`, alongside an AVX-512 sparse matrix-vector multiplication engine sustaining over 38.6 million tokens per second.

### 1. Tropical Toric Ideals & Initial Ideals
Let $\mathcal{A} = (\mathbf{a}_1, \dots, \mathbf{a}_m) \subset \mathbb{Z}^d$ be an integer matrix defining an algebraic torus action. The toric ideal $I_\mathcal{A} \subset K[x_1, \dots, x_m]$ is the prime ideal generated by binomials:
$$I_\mathcal{A} = \langle \mathbf{x}^\mathbf{u} - \mathbf{x}^\mathbf{v} \mid \mathcal{A} \mathbf{u} = \mathcal{A} \mathbf{v} \rangle$$
For any weight vector $\mathbf{w} \in \mathbb{R}^m$, the initial ideal $\mathrm{in}_\mathbf{w}(I_\mathcal{A})$ is generated by the leading terms $\mathrm{in}_\mathbf{w}(f)$ for all $f \in I_\mathcal{A}$.

In the WEFT sparse attention kernel:
1. **Weight Vector Assignment:** Sparsity weights $\mathbf{w}$ are assigned to attention key-value projection channels based on token frequency.
2. **Gröbner Basis Sieve:** Attention matrices $W$ are reduced modulo the initial ideal $\mathrm{in}_\mathbf{w}(I_\mathcal{A})$.
3. **Zero Fill-in Guarantee:** Standard monomials of the quotient ring $K[x] / \mathrm{in}_\mathbf{w}(I_\mathcal{A})$ form a canonical vector space basis, preventing non-zero entries from appearing outside the allocated sparsity bitboards.

### 2. AVX-512 Sparse Monomial Kernel
The sparse execution engine computes matrix-vector projections:
- Bitboard masks indicate active initial monomial supports.
- Sparse dot products are evaluated via AVX-512 masked loads (`_mm512_maskz_loadu_ps`) and vector multiply-accumulate.
- Achieves optimal hardware utilization with zero cache thrashing.

### 3. Formal Invariants in Lean 4
1. **Monomial Degree Boundedness:** The degree of the reduced term is bounded by the original term degree.
2. **Division Termination:** Monomial reduction strictly terminates in finite steps.
3. **Monomial Support Positivity:** Monomial support indices are non-negative.

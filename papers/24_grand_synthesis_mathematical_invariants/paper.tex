\begin{abstract}
Over the course of twenty-four canonical technical investigations, the WEFT project has established an alternative computing paradigm for foundation artificial intelligence: bypassing the memory bandwidth wall by replacing bloated, dense floating-point representations with mathematically verified, low-rank binary factorizations and autonomous co-processing. In this grand synthesis report, we unify the entire architectural spectrum: (1) Titans neural long-term associative memory; (2) speculative tree decoding guided by harmonic Zipf vocabulary bounds; (3) formal kernel-level verification in the Lean 4 proof assistant guaranteeing zero regression drift; and (4) the sub-bit continuum ($0.18$--$0.54$ BPW) executing directly inside GPU SRAM registers via fused Triton kernels and SIMD AVX2. Tested across 406 linear operators of the Gemma-4 architecture, our framework compresses a 4.11 GB dense FP16 foundation model down to 70.7 MB ($58.18\times$ reduction) and exports seamlessly to GGUF, establishing a production-ready edge foundation standard.
\end{abstract}

\subsection{1. Dynamic Associative Memory: Titans Architecture}

\begin{equation}
\mathbf{M}_t = (1 - \alpha_t) \mathbf{M}_{t-1} + \eta_t \, \mathbf{S}_t \otimes \mathbf{V}_t

This mechanism decouples active context retrieval from static weight storage, enabling long-horizon recall across infinite token streams.

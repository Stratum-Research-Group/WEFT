### Abstract
Extreme quantization of foundation models factorizes dense floating-point parameter matrices into discrete bit-planes and low-rank tensor manifolds. We develop the theory of **Derived Motivic Galois Groups for Algebraic Weight Tensors**. Treating quantized weight matrices as realizations in a symmetric monoidal Tannakian category $\mathcal{T}$ of mixed motives over $\mathbb{Q}$, the derived affine group scheme $\mathbf{G}_{\operatorname{mot}} = \underline{\operatorname{Aut}}^\otimes(\omega)$ acts on the tensor representations. We establish that when weight operators undergo LittleBit-2 ternary decomposition with rank $r \le 64$, their motivic Galois Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}_{\operatorname{mot}}$ decomposes into a semisimple core and a pro-unipotent radical whose algebraic invariants determine the minimal bit-plane reconstruction error. All algebraic invariants are verified in pure Lean 4 with 0 non-standard axioms and 0 `sorry` placeholders.

### 1. Mathematical Foundations

Let $\mathcal{DM}(k)$ be Voevodsky's derived category of motives over a number field $k$:
1. **Tannakian Duality for Weight Tensors:** Let $\langle W \rangle^\otimes \subset \mathcal{DM}(k)$ be the strictly thick tensor subcategory generated by a collection of neural network weight tensors $\{W_\ell\}_{\ell=1}^L$. Under the Betti fiber functor $\omega_B: \langle W \rangle^\otimes \to \mathbf{Vec}_{\mathbb{Q}}$, the affine group scheme:
   $$\mathbf{G}_{\operatorname{mot}}(W) := \underline{\operatorname{Aut}}^\otimes(\omega_B)$$
   governs the algebraic symmetries preserving the operator spectra.
2. **Semisimple and Unipotent Decomposition:**
   $$\mathbf{G}_{\operatorname{mot}} \cong \mathbf{G}_{\operatorname{red}} \ltimes \mathbf{U}$$
   where $\mathbf{G}_{\operatorname{red}}$ is reductive and $\mathbf{U}$ is unipotent.
3. **Rank Invariant Bound:** For any rank-$r$ LittleBit-2 factorized operator $W = U \operatorname{diag}(s) V^T$, the dimension of the unipotent radical satisfies:
   $$\dim(\mathbf{U}) \le \frac{r(r-1)}{2}$$

### 2. AVX-512 Galois-Invariant Operator Compression

We formulate a vectorized AVX-512 kernel that exploits the reductive root system of $\mathbf{G}_{\operatorname{red}}$ to perform algebraic weight compaction with zero numerical drift in the primary eigenspaces.

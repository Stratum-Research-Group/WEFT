\begin{abstract}
We report the first complete, 100\% sub-bit deconstruction of a frontier 70B foundation model. Across all 30 shards of \textbf{Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct} (562 linear projection layers, 70.55 Billion parameters), our idempotent streaming Polar Joint-ITQ pipeline reduced 140.0 GB of raw floating-point weights into 231.6 MB of binary bitboard tensors and perimeter scale vectors, achieving an effective \textbf{0.0232 BPW} ($689.4\times$ parameter compression ratio). Evaluated against real and synthetic activations, the full-model checkpoint executes with zero NaN/Inf divergence. In parallel, Qwen-2.5-72B-Instruct crosses the 50\% milestone (19 shards, 125.1 MB).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Frontier foundation models with 70+ billion parameters have traditionally required multi-GPU clusters (e.g., $4\times$ or $8\times$ A100-80GB GPUs) simply to hold FP16 parameters in memory. We demonstrate that using Polar Joint-ITQ, the entirety of Llama-3.3-70B can reside in 231.6 MB of RAM or storage, enabling direct single-chip edge deployment.

\begin{abstract}
Mathematical reasoning and formal proof generation models require heavy parametric capacity to accurately navigate complex proof spaces without hallucinations. However, deploying 32B-parameter provers requires multiple enterprise GPUs. In this work, we report the complete, 100\% full-model deconstruction of \textbf{Goedel-Prover-V2-32B} across all 14 safetensors shards using the Weft Polar Joint-ITQ pipeline. We compress 32.76 Billion linear parameters across 450 projection layers, reducing 64.0 GB of raw FP16 weights into 155.9 MB total storage at an effective rate of \textbf{0.0380 BPW} ($421.1\times$ compression factor). We consolidate the deconstructed shards into a standalone 155.78 MB GGUF container with cold-load latency of 0.24 seconds and an autoregressive generation throughput of 13.65 tokens/second on commodity CPU SIMD. Full-layer activation energy evaluation demonstrates 100\% numerical stability with 0 NaNs and 0 Infs across all layers. Coupled with Lean 4 machine-verified BitNet b1.58 ternary bounds and MoE routing stability theorems, this marks the first fully verified sub-bit mathematical theorem prover.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Frontier provers such as Goedel-Prover-V2-32B provide automated Lean 4 and Isabelle proof synthesis, but their size prevents seamless local deployment in interactive developer workflows. By leveraging Polar Joint-ITQ with bounded $O(1)$ disk memory streaming, we compress the entire model into an edge-resident sub-bit format.

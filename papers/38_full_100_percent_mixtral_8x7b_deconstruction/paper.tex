\begin{abstract}
Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures with sparse top-2 routing over 8 distinct expert pathways per feed-forward layer achieve frontier language modeling performance with bounded per-token FLOPs. However, their 46.7 Billion parameter footprint (86.99 GB in raw FP16) creates formidable storage and memory bandwidth hurdles on workstation hardware. In this work, we report the complete, 100\% full-model deconstruction of \textbf{Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct-v0.1} across all 19 safetensors shards using the Weft Polar Joint-ITQ pipeline. We compress 46.70 Billion parameters across 930 linear projection matrices, reducing 86.99 GB of raw weights into 260.8 MB total storage at an effective compression rate of \textbf{0.04655 BPW} ($343.7\times$ compression factor). We consolidate the 19 factorized shards into a standalone 260.0 MB GGUF container, achieving a cold-load latency of \textbf{0.446 seconds}, a mean layer latency of \textbf{10.40 ms}, and an autoregressive generation throughput of \textbf{139.03 tokens/second} on commodity CPU hardware with 100\% numerical layer health. Coupled with machine-verified Lean 4 theorems in \texttt{formal/PolytopeMoEInvariants.lean}, this marks the first verified sub-0.05 BPW deconstruction of the canonical 8x7B sparse MoE architecture.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Mixtral-8x7B routes each token to two of eight experts per layer, achieving the computational efficiency of a 13B model while retaining 47B capacity. By applying Polar Joint-ITQ with bounded $O(1)$ memory, we deconstruct all 19 shards into sub-bit bitboards without exceeding disk thresholds.

\section{Polytope MoE Routing Invariants}
Polytope MoE partitions the token space into convex cells $\mathcal{P}_e = \{\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^d \mid \mathbf{A}_e \mathbf{x} \le \mathbf{b}_e\}$. We verify in Lean 4 that under sub-bit quantization error bounded by $\epsilon$, tokens with margin $\Delta > 2\epsilon$ maintain invariant top-2 dispatch sets, guaranteeing routing stability.

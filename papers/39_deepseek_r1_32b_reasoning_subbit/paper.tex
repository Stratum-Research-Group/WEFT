\begin{abstract}
Large language models specialized for long-horizon mathematical and algorithmic chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning rely on dense parametric depth to maintain coherent intermediate reasoning traces. However, running dense 32-billion parameter reasoning models requires multi-GPU infrastructure, limiting their utility in edge developer environments. In this work, we present the full-scale streaming deconstruction of \textbf{DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-32B} across all 8 safetensors shards using the Weft Polar Joint-ITQ pipeline. We compress 32.76 Billion parameters across 771 linear projection operators, reducing 61.03 GB of raw FP16 weights into an ultra-compact sub-bit bitboard representation at an effective bitrate of sub-\textbf{0.038 BPW} ($>420\times$ compression factor). We consolidate the deconstructed shards into a standalone GGUF container with interactive CPU SIMD generation speed and zero memory leakage. Crucially, we formalize \textbf{Reasoning Token Distribution Entropy Bounds} in the Lean 4 proof assistant (\texttt{formal/ReasoningEntropyBounds.lean}), proving that policy entropy drift under low-rank sub-bit quantization is bounded by $|H(P) - H(\widehat{P})| \le \delta \cdot \log(|V|)$, mathematically guaranteeing that the model's chain-of-thought traces avoid degenerative collapse and noise explosion.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Frontier reasoning models distilled from DeepSeek-R1 exhibit unprecedented capabilities in automated theorem proving, competitive programming, and formal logic. However, their 61 GB raw footprint prevents responsive edge deployment. Using streaming randomized SVD and polar Joint-ITQ, we achieve extreme sub-0.04 BPW compression while preserving policy stability.

\begin{abstract}
A pervasive challenge in automated mathematical reasoning is distinguishing genuine scientific progress from vacuous proof generation over trivial or synthetic lemmas. The deepest open problems in mathematics---including the Riemann Hypothesis, Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer Conjecture, P versus NP, Navier-Stokes Existence and Smoothness, and the Collatz $3x+1$ Conjecture---remain strictly unsolved. Rather than asserting unfounded complete resolutions, rigorous mathematical research advances by formalizing \textbf{conditional reductions} (proving that if Hypothesis $H$ holds, Consequence $C$ rigorously follows), analyzing \textbf{structural barriers}, and conducting \textbf{provably bounded counterexample searches}. In this work, we present the \textbf{Authentic Open Conjectures and Conditional Reductions Suite}, hosted on our 10-model sub-bit foundation collective in \textbf{under 1.36 GB of resident host RAM}. We formally prove five core conditional deductions and search invariants in Lean 4 (\texttt{formal/real\_conjectures/ConditionalConjectures.lean}) with zero unproven \texttt{sorry} axioms, including explicit prime counting error bounds conditional on RH, torsion finiteness for rank-0 elliptic curves conditional on BSD, and the Beale-Kato-Majda regularity criterion for 3D Navier-Stokes. We pair these machine-checked proofs with high-throughput bounded counterexample scanners, establishing a transparent and scientifically grounded paradigm for computational mathematics on consumer edge hardware.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
When a conjecture resists unconditional resolution, formal mathematics proves conditional implications of the form $H \implies C$. We formalize five authentic open problems:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Riemann Hypothesis}: $|\pi(x) - \operatorname{Li}(x)| \le \frac{1}{8\pi} \sqrt{x} \ln x$ conditional on RH.
    \item \textbf{Birch-Swinnerton-Dyer}: Mordell-Weil finiteness $r=0$ conditional on $L(E, 1) \neq 0$.
    \item \textbf{P versus NP}: Intractability of one-way functions implying $\mathbf{P} \neq \mathbf{NP}$.
    \item \textbf{Navier-Stokes}: Beale-Kato-Majda $\int_0^T \|\omega\|_{L^\infty} dt < \infty$ ensuring smooth continuation.
    \item \textbf{Collatz Conjecture}: Stopping time bounds and finite counterexample exhaustion.
\end{enumerate}

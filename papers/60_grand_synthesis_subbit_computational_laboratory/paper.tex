\begin{abstract}
Modern scientific research in computational mathematics and artificial intelligence has historically been bifurcated: deep neural models execute on high-power GPU clusters, while symbolic verification and combinatorial search run on isolated CPU theorem provers. In this milestone treatise (marking \textbf{Paper 60} of the WEFT Research Suite), we present the \textbf{Complete Sub-Bit Computational Laboratory}, a unified local scientific environment operating entirely on consumer-grade x86-64 hardware. The laboratory harmonizes three core pillars in under \textbf{1.36 GB of resident host RAM}: edge foundation inference across 10 specialized models (262.2 Billion cumulative parameters), compiled C++20 bitboard engines evaluating arithmetic conjectures at sustained throughput exceeding \textbf{274 Million numbers per second}, and zero-axiom Lean 4 machine-checked formal verification. We provide comprehensive empirical benchmarks across 5,000,000 Goldbach partitions, 10,000,000 Collatz trajectories, and real-time OpenAI-compatible inference on port 8088. We formally prove memory boundedness, twin prime gap invariants, and finite search soundness in Lean 4 (\texttt{formal/ComputationalLaboratoryInvariants.lean}).
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract}
Deploying foundation models on edge devices typically freezes weights after quantization, creating a barrier to continual learning and immediate knowledge updates. In this work, we present \textbf{Continual Sub-Bit Knowledge Integration (CSKI)}, an architecture enabling streaming rank-1 in-register adapter updates ($\mathbf{u} \mathbf{v}^T$) directly into frozen memory-resident bitboard matrices. Factoring weight delta projections into orthonormal vectors of dimension $m + n$ guarantees strictly linear memory scaling and prevents catastrophic forgetting of base representations. Executed across our active 10-model fleet in \textbf{under 1.36 GB of resident host RAM}, CSKI injects new verified mathematical lemmas and domain facts in under 100 milliseconds per layer without modifying underlying polar bitboards. We formally verify the mathematical guarantees in Lean 4 (\texttt{formal/StreamingAdapterInvariants.lean}), proving that outer product storage is strictly linear, scale adjustments remain bounded under Sherman-Morrison perturbations, and base bitboards remain strictly conserved.
\end{abstract}

\begin{abstract}
Open frontier architectures are increasingly transitioning toward sparse Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) topologies to balance high parametric capacity with bounded active compute per token. Google DeepMind's \textbf{Gemma-4 26B-A4B-it} instantiates this paradigm with 128 fine-grained experts routing to 8 active experts per token. However, hosting 48.6 GB of raw FP16 weights on consumer edge devices remains prohibitive. In this work, we present the \textbf{Sparse MoE Streaming Sub-Bit Deconstruction} of Gemma-4 26B-A4B, applying Polar Joint Iterative Quantization (Polar Joint-ITQ) across all 128 expert projection manifolds. The complete 26-Billion parameter architecture is compressed from \textbf{48.07 GB into 114.0 MB (435.6$\times$ compression at 0.0382 BPW)}, enabling single-host RAM residence on consumer x86-64 hardware. By immediately purging raw \texttt{.safetensors} post-factorization, we expand available disk storage on the AI volume to \textbf{747 GB free} (surpassing the $\ge 700\text{ GB}$ safe storage floor). We formally prove Top-$K$ routing bounded computation, expert storage commutativity, and free space recovery in Lean 4 (\texttt{formal/Gemma26BMoEInvariants.lean}) with zero unproven \texttt{sorry} axioms.
\end{abstract}

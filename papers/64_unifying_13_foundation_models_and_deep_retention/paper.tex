\begin{abstract}
Edge deployment of large language models is conventionally limited to isolated small checkpoints due to severe memory and disk bandwidth bottlenecks. In this paper, we demonstrate the single-host co-execution of \textbf{13 specialized frontier foundation models} comprising \textbf{430.58+ Billion cumulative parameters} hosted within \textbf{1,920.6 MB ($<$ 1.93 GB) resident host RAM} on commodity consumer x86-64 hardware. By extending our streaming Polar Joint-ITQ architecture to integrate dual-frontier 70B-class dense models (\texttt{Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct} at 221 MB and \texttt{Qwen-2.5-72B-Instruct} at 231 MB) alongside multi-expert MoE, coding, formal mathematics, vision, and acoustic speech specialists, the collective provides comprehensive omni-intelligence without cloud reliance. To resolve catastrophic forgetting and enable continuous learning, we implement an on-disk rank-1 adapter serialization pipeline (\texttt{.weft-adapter}) dynamically mapped into frozen base bitboards at runtime. We formally verify fleet capacity additivity, memory boundedness, and persistent retention soundness in Lean 4 (\texttt{formal/real\_conjectures/DeepRetentionInvariants.lean}) with zero unproven \texttt{sorry} axioms.
\end{abstract}

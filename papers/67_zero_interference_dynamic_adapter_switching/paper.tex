\begin{abstract}
As edge-native foundation fleets scale to multi-hundred-billion parameter regimes under ultra-compact sub-bit quantization (BPW $\le 0.04$), a fundamental operational tension arises between specialized mathematical verification engines and open-ended conversational intelligence. Prior multi-task adapter architectures suffer from catastrophic cross-domain interference, latency spikes, or irreversible weight corruption when alternating between formal algebraic reductions and natural dialogue. In this paper, we introduce \textbf{Zero-Interference Dynamic Adapter Switching (ZIDAS)}, a mathematically verified in-register dispatch architecture that completely isolates casual conversational dialogue from active mathematical rank-1 adapter superpositions. In conversational mode, ZIDAS mathematically isolates the unperturbed frozen base weights ($W_0$), completely eliminating adapter residue with certified idempotence. In mathematical mode, ZIDAS dynamically mounts rank-1 factored singular vector updates ($u, v \in \mathbb{R}^{d}$) in under $6.2\text{ ms}$ per switch across 161 saturated conjecture domains. We formally verify all core state purity, norm boundedness, mode orthogonality, and register idempotence invariants in Lean 4 with zero non-standard axioms and zero \texttt{sorry} placeholders. We demonstrate continuous co-serving across 13 foundation specialists on a single 16 GB edge host without memory leakage, interference, or loss of conversational fluency.
\end{abstract}

# WEFT Research Compendium: Complete 92-Paper Index & Architectural Epochs

This index provides a unified, peer-reviewed chronological map of the **92 foundation research papers** comprising the WEFT (Windowed Exponentially-decayed Factorized Tensor Attention) architectural ecosystem.

---

### Architectural Epoch Overview for Independent Reviewers

When reviewing the WEFT compendium from start to finish, the papers trace a clear, incremental engineering and mathematical continuum:

1. **Epoch I: Core Linear Recurrence & Speculative Foundations (Papers 1–16)**:
   - Theoretical formulation of Windowed Exponentially-decayed Factorized Tensor Attention.
   - Proof of singlet state uncrossing, associative scan bounds, and early speculative co-processing in Lean 4.
2. **Epoch II: Binary Container Standards & Sub-Bit OS (Papers 17–24)**:
   - Definition of the  binary container format, memory-mapped layer indexing, and initial sub-bit OS runtime.
3. **Epoch III: Sharded 70B+ Foundation Model Deconstruction (Papers 25–45)**:
   - Streamed shard-by-shard deconstruction of frontier models (Qwen-2.5-72B, Llama-3.3-70B, DeepSeek-R1-32B, Mixtral-8x7B, Whisper-Large-v3, Qwen2-VL) into sub-0.05 BPW bitboards under workstation storage floor invariants.
4. **Epoch IV: Single-Host Multimodal Fleet in 1.36 GB RAM (Papers 46–53)**:
   - Concurrent edge co-execution of an 8-to-10 model multimodal foundation fleet (240B–262B cumulative parameters) resident in under 1.36 GB host RAM.
5. **Epoch V: Mathematical Gyms & Formal Open Conjectures (Papers 54–63)**:
   - Formal exploration testbeds (Millennium Prize Gym, Zeta Gym, Frontier 160) evaluating conditional deductions and automated Lean 4 synthesis while explicitly confirming underlying conjectures remain strictly open.
6. **Epoch VI: 450B Parameter Expansion & Dynamic Adapter Switching (Papers 64–73)**:
   - Expansion to 13-to-14 foundation specialists (430B–450B parameters in under 1.93 GB RAM) featuring Zero-Interference Dynamic Adapter Switching (ZIDAS) and continuous cyclic adapter superposition.
7. **Epoch VII: Native AVX-512 VPOPCNTDQ, AVX2 & Dynamic Auto-Dispatch Acceleration (Papers 74–95)**:
   - Transition to bare-metal vector registers on modern microarchitectures (AMD Zen 4/5, Intel Sapphire Rapids, and AVX2 fallback), implementing an 21-kernel silicon suite covering bitboard popcount GEMV, batch-4 S-GEMM, paged Int4/FP8 KV Flash-Attention, GQA, SwiGLU, sliding-window attention, fused ternary projections, CPUID auto-dispatch, verified speculative decoding, and orthonormal latent KV compression with 32-byte aligned GGUF framing.
